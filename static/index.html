Data notes

    How to read this comparison +

    Return & benchmark

    Total return is the change in Norgate’s dividend-adjusted close. Excess return is the percentage-point difference from your benchmark ETF. Growth starts every series at 100.

    Risk & alignment

    Volatility uses the sample standard deviation of daily returns × √252. Drawdown measures the decline from the highest value within this window. All symbols use common observed dates; missing prices are never filled.

    Evidence, with limits

    This is a historical comparison of selected symbols, not a trading strategy or an unbiased stock-selection backtest. It excludes taxes, trading costs, and execution effects. Today’s watchlist can introduce survivorship and selection bias.
